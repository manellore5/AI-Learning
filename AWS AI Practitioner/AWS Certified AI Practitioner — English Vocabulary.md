# AWS Certified AI Practitioner — English Vocabulary
Course: AWS AI Practitioner · Added: 2026-06-30 · src-id: aws-aif-english-vocab

> 63 less-common English words drawn from the presentation (slides 1–368), packed into 13 cards (4–5 words each) in the deck `AWS Certified AI Practitioner::English Vocabulary`. Front lists the words; back gives meanings. Excludes AWS/AI jargon (that lives in the Glossary).


**Card 1:** Accountability · Acknowledge · Adherence · Aerospace · Anomaly

- **Accountability** — being responsible for your actions and required to explain or justify them.
- **Acknowledge** — to accept or admit that something is true or exists.
- **Adherence** — the act of following a rule, standard, or agreement closely.
- **Aerospace** — the industry concerned with building and flying aircraft and spacecraft.
- **Anomaly** — something that deviates from what is normal or expected.

**Card 2:** Apprenticeship · Approximation · Arbitrary · Assertion · Autonomous

- **Apprenticeship** — a period of learning a skill or trade by working under an expert.
- **Approximation** — a value or result that is close to, but not exactly, the true one.
- **Arbitrary** — based on random choice or personal whim rather than reason.
- **Assertion** — a confident and forceful statement that something is true.
- **Autonomous** — able to operate and make decisions independently.

**Card 3:** Brevity · Cadence · Censorship · Circumvent · Cognitive

- **Brevity** — the quality of using few words; shortness.
- **Cadence** — a regular rhythm, pace, or recurring pattern of an activity.
- **Censorship** — the suppression or removal of content deemed unacceptable.
- **Circumvent** — to cleverly find a way around a rule or obstacle.
- **Cognitive** — relating to thinking, reasoning, and understanding.

**Card 4:** Coherence · Conservative · Consortium · Constitute · Controllability

- **Coherence** — the quality of being logical, consistent, and well-connected.
- **Conservative** — cautious; avoiding risk or extremes.
- **Consortium** — a group of companies or institutions cooperating for a shared goal.
- **Constitute** — to be or make up (something).
- **Controllability** — the degree to which something can be directed or kept in check.

**Card 5:** Crowdsourcing · Culprit · Cumulative · Curate · Custodian

- **Crowdsourcing** — obtaining work, ideas, or content from a large online crowd.
- **Culprit** — the person or thing responsible for a problem or fault.
- **Cumulative** — increasing gradually by successive additions.
- **Curate** — to carefully select, organize, and present.
- **Custodian** — a person responsible for looking after or guarding something.

**Card 6:** Deterministic · Disproportionately · Disturbing · Dynamism · Emergent

- **Deterministic** — having outcomes fully fixed by the inputs, with no randomness.
- **Disproportionately** — to a degree that is too large or unequal relative to something.
- **Disturbing** — causing anxiety, worry, or distress.
- **Dynamism** — the quality of being energetic, forceful, and constantly changing.
- **Emergent** — arising or becoming apparent, often newly or unexpectedly.

**Card 7:** Empathetic · Emulate · Enforce · Escalation · Expedited

- **Empathetic** — showing the ability to understand and share others' feelings.
- **Emulate** — to match or imitate, especially by copying behavior.
- **Enforce** — to compel obedience to a rule or law.
- **Escalation** — an increase in intensity, seriousness, or extent.
- **Expedited** — made faster; sped up.

**Card 8:** Granular · Illicit · Inherent · Intuitive · Jargon

- **Granular** — composed of fine detail; highly detailed.
- **Illicit** — forbidden by law, rules, or custom.
- **Inherent** — existing as a natural, permanent, and inseparable part of something.
- **Intuitive** — easy to understand or use without conscious reasoning.
- **Jargon** — specialized words used by a particular profession or group.

**Card 9:** Lineage · Malicious · Mitigate · Nondeterminism · Nuance

- **Lineage** — the line of origin or descent; the documented history of where something came from.
- **Malicious** — intending to do harm.
- **Mitigate** — to make something less severe, serious, or harmful.
- **Nondeterminism** — the property of not producing the same output every time.
- **Nuance** — a subtle difference in meaning, expression, or quality.

**Card 10:** Obfuscation · Occluded · Opacity · Perpetuate · Plagiarism

- **Obfuscation** — the act of making something unclear or hard to understand.
- **Occluded** — blocked, hidden, or obstructed from view.
- **Opacity** — the state of being hard to see through, or hard to understand.
- **Perpetuate** — to cause something (often undesirable) to continue indefinitely.
- **Plagiarism** — presenting someone else's work or ideas as your own.

**Card 11:** Plausible · Precedent · Redaction · Reflexivity · Remediation

- **Plausible** — seeming reasonable, believable, or probable.
- **Precedent** — an earlier event or decision used as an example or guide for later ones.
- **Redaction** — the removal or blacking-out of sensitive information from a document.
- **Reflexivity** — the practice of reflecting on one's own decision-making process.
- **Remediation** — the act of correcting or fixing a fault or problem.

**Card 12:** Resilience · Robustness · Sporadic · Steward

- **Resilience** — the ability to recover quickly from difficulties or failures.
- **Robustness** — the quality of being strong and able to withstand adverse conditions.
- **Sporadic** — happening at irregular intervals; occasional.
- **Steward** — a person who manages or takes responsible care of something.

**Card 13:** Subsidiary · Superficial · Tailored · Veracity

- **Subsidiary** — a company controlled by another, larger (parent) company.
- **Superficial** — shallow; lacking depth or thoroughness.
- **Tailored** — customized to particular needs or requirements.
- **Veracity** — conformity to truth; accuracy.
